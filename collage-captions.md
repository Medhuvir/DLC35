## Notes for copy editor

- **Thumbnail Caption** renders at 10px, tilted with the photo card. Keep it tight — a name, a date, a short label.
- **Modal Caption** renders at 13px centered below the full-size photo in the lightbox. Can be a complete sentence.
- Both fields are independent — they do not need to match.
- Thumbnail supports a hard line break (use a `<br>` in the final HTML if two distinct lines are needed, otherwise it wraps naturally).
